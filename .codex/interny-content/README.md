# Interny TikTok content system

## Project context

Interny helps high school students discover and track verified internships. This folder is the project-local source of truth for TikTok references, audits, templates, and generated examples.

## Imported reference posts

- `reference-posts/cornell-stats/`: two-slide admissions-stat carousel supplied as `Stats That Got This Student Into Cornell (1).zip`.
- `reference-posts/five-minute/`: five-slide internship-advice carousel supplied as `A 5-Minute (27).zip`.

## Why the current posts are likely stalling

### Admissions-stat post

- The archive says Cornell, while the cover says Stanford. That mismatch weakens trust immediately.
- The hook is recognizable but too generic. It does not promise a surprising takeaway or give a reason to save/share.
- Slide two is a wall of text with weak scan order and several editing issues, including duplicated parentheses.
- Raw stats invite comparison, but the post gives no analysis, context, or takeaway.
- Two slides are too few to build curiosity and completion momentum.

### Five-minute internship post

- The opening question is broad and reads like a presentation title, not a TikTok-native hook.
- Slides one and two repeat generic advice instead of delivering a concrete win.
- The sequence skips slide 3 and labels the final two slides as 4.
- Interny is not shown until the last slide, where its value is buried in a long paragraph.
- There is no product screenshot, screen recording, proof, specific internship, deadline, or student result.
- The notebook styling is attractive but low-contrast and too editorial for fast mobile scanning.

## New content rules

1. Make the first slide a specific promise, warning, number, or contrarian claim.
2. Keep each slide to one idea and roughly 8 to 22 words, excluding small metadata.
3. Show proof by slide two: a real listing, real app screen, verified URL, deadline, or outcome.
4. Reveal Interny naturally by slide two or three, not in a final sales paragraph.
5. Use 4 to 7 slides with visible progress such as `1/3`.
6. Use large sans-serif display text, strong contrast, and one accent color. Keep the notebook texture as a brand cue, not the main event.
7. End with one action only: save, comment a field, share with a friend, or use the link in bio.
8. Never post unverified deadlines, eligibility rules, acceptance claims, or student profiles.
9. Export a clean 4:5 carousel and also test a 9:16 talking-head or screen-record version of the same concept.
10. Judge formats over at least five posts using hold rate, completion rate, saves, shares, profile visits, and app-store clicks, not views alone.

## Best repeatable series

- **Apply This Week:** three verified opportunities with deadline, grade, location, pay, and one reason each is worth applying to.
- **No Experience Needed:** beginner-friendly internships filtered by grade or field.
- **Deadline Radar:** opportunities closing in 7, 14, and 30 days.
- **Internship Myth Check:** correct one misconception with an app listing as proof.
- **What I Would Apply To:** founder-led weekly picks with a candid face-to-camera intro.
- **Application Teardown:** improve a weak resume bullet, cold email, or short answer in under 30 seconds.
- **Stats, Then Strategy:** admissions profile followed by three useful lessons and a clear disclaimer that one profile is not a formula.
- **Hidden Filter:** demonstrate one Interny filter against a specific student scenario.
- **Parent/Counselor Send:** a useful checklist designed to be shared with a student.
- **Build in Public:** show how one internship was verified and added to Interny.

## Example six-slide carousel

1. `3 HIGH SCHOOL INTERNSHIPS YOU SHOULD APPLY TO THIS WEEK` / `SAVE THIS ->`
2. `1/3 [PROGRAM NAME]` plus deadline, eligibility, pay, and location in four short chips.
3. `WHY IT'S WORTH IT` plus one concrete benefit and one honest competitiveness note.
4. Repeat slides two and three for the strongest second/third programs, or use one compact card per program.
5. `I CHECKED THE DETAILS SO YOU DON'T HAVE TO` over a screen recording or screenshot of the Interny listing and filters.
6. `COMMENT YOUR FIELD FOR NEXT WEEK'S LIST` with a small `Find 700+ verified opportunities on Interny` line.

Use only live, freshly verified program details when replacing placeholders.
