# Photography prompts

The two cover photographs were made with the built-in image-generation tool. All typography and carousel layouts were then rendered deterministically so copy, spacing, and brand colors remain controlled.

## Internship search cover

Natural editorial, over-the-shoulder photograph of a high school student searching for internships on a laptop at a believable bedroom study desk. Late-afternoon window light, warm neutrals, slate navy, restrained blue detail, and generous negative space for typography. No readable screen text, logos, illustration, cartoon styling, exaggerated expression, stickers, or glossy ad treatment.

## College profile cover

Restrained documentary editorial still life of a student application workspace with blurred résumé pages, laptop edge, black pen, deep-red clothbound notebook, and a softly blurred stone university building beyond a library window. Ivory, charcoal, oxblood, and gray. No readable document text, university logos, people, graduation symbols, illustration, cartoon styling, or glossy ad treatment.
