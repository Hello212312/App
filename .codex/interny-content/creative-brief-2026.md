# Interny social creative brief

## Required visual direction

- Premium editorial briefing, not an illustrated advertisement.
- Real app UI, real listing evidence, restrained photography, and candid process footage.
- Interny palette from the app: `#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`, `#0F172A`, `#64748B`, and `#2563EB`.
- System/Inter-style sans serif, strong grid, generous margins, and one message per slide.
- No AI-looking faces, cartoons, fake stickers, marker circles, neon gradients, or glossy 3D objects.
- Every carousel deliverable must include every slide.

## Research basis

TikTok's 2026 trend forecast emphasizes grounded stories, real process, curiosity-led discovery, and demonstrable everyday value. TikTok's creative guidance consistently recommends an immediate hook, early value, readable text, people or product proof, native vertical composition, and a single clear close.

## Controlled test

Test genuinely different hooks and evidence structures, not color swaps. Keep the topic constant when testing a cover so the visual treatment is the variable. Evaluate saves, shares, completion, profile visits, and app-store clicks alongside views.
